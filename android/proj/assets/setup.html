Jade Link

Lets your Jade (jade-py) use this phone: calls and texts, the screen, the camera and sensors, and Sentry.

Connect to jade-py off

The phone keeps a connection open to your jade serve. Works on Wi-Fi and mobile data, as long as the phone can reach it (same network, or a VPN such as Tailscale).

Connect

Let jade-py connect to this phone off

Or the other way round: Jade Link listens, and jade-py calls in. Use it on your own network or over a VPN; it is plain HTTP.

Listen
Reachable from the network (off: this phone only) 

  






  
Talk and kiosk off

  
Talk mode is in the dashboard (the Talk button): she listens, answers out loud and listens again, with only her avatar on screen. Kiosk mode makes this device Jade's: Jade Link becomes the home screen, full screen and pinned in front, for a tablet on a wall or a spare phone.

  
Start talk mode when it opens
the dashboard opens straight into hands-free talking

  
Kiosk mode
home screen, full screen, always in front

  
For a real lock (no way out without turning it off here), make Jade Link the device owner once, from a computer, on a freshly reset device: adb shell dpm set-device-owner app.netrunner.jadelink/app.netrunner.jade.AdminReceiver. Without that, Android's screen pinning keeps it in front.
